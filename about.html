Our story

From one van in Birmingham to 480 partners nationwide

In 2016, Dan Okafor and Chris Reilly were subcontracting for a national breakdown brand. They saw the same thing every shift: drivers stuck for hours, quoted one price on the phone and charged another on the kerb, and told "we can't do keys" or "that'll need a tow" when a £70 roadside fix would have done it.

So they started RoadRescue with a single kitted van and a rule that still governs everything: tell the customer the real price before you touch the car, and try to fix it where it stands.

Today that rule is enforced across a vetted network of 480+ independent engineers and auto locksmiths covering every UK postcode area, coordinated from a control room in Birmingham that never closes.

  • No membership fees — 100% pay-as-you-go
  • 94% of callouts resolved without a tow
  • UK-staffed control room, not an overseas call centre

What we stand for

Four promises we don't bend

Price honesty

A fixed quote, in writing, before work begins. If the job turns out smaller, you pay less — that has happened 3,100 times.

Speed with safety

Fast matters, but nobody works on a live carriageway without cones, lights and a risk assessment. Ever.

Fix, don't tow

Towing is the lazy answer. Our vans carry the diagnostics, keys kit and parts to finish the job where you are.

Human on the phone

A named UK dispatcher owns your callout from first ring to “you're back on the road”.

Our journey

Milestones

2016

One van, one promise

Dan and Chris launch RoadRescue in Birmingham with a single Transit and a fixed-price pledge.

2018

Auto locksmith division

Key cutting and transponder programming added after 1 in 5 calls turned out to be a key problem.

2020

Key-worker support

Free callouts for NHS staff through the pandemic — 4,200 jobs completed at no charge.

2022

National network

Partner model launched; coverage reaches every UK postcode area and Northern Ireland.

2024

Live dispatch platform

GPS matching and WhatsApp status updates cut average arrival time from 41 to 27 minutes.

2026

Electrifying the fleet

First 40 electric service vans on the road; EV and hybrid high-voltage training rolled out network-wide.
