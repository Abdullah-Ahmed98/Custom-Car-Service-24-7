Roadside safety

If you break down on a motorway

Highways England data shows the hard shoulder is one of the most dangerous places in the UK. Follow this order — every time, even in the rain.

Step 1

Pull as far left as possible

Get onto the hard shoulder or into an emergency refuge area, wheels turned to the left, and switch on your hazard lights immediately.

Step 2

Everyone out on the passenger side

Exit through the left-hand doors, including pets on leads, and climb behind the safety barrier. Never stand between the car and traffic.

Step 3

Move up the embankment and back

Stand behind the barrier and upstream of your vehicle so you are not in the path if it is struck.

Step 4

Call for help — 999 first if in a live lane

If you cannot leave a live running lane, stay belted in with hazards on and call 999. Otherwise call us or use the orange SOS phone.

Step 5

Do not attempt repairs yourself

No changing a wheel, no lifting the bonnet, no warning triangle on a motorway. Wait behind the barrier until your engineer arrives.
